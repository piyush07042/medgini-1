Why LLM? & Explainable AI (XAI)

1. Natural Language Reasoning: Traditional machine learning algorithms (like XGBoost) calculate numeric statistical probabilities (e.g., 75% risk score). However, doctors require clinical context, guidelines, and patient-specific rationales. LLMs synthesize these metrics into a coherent clinical summary.

2. RAG Grounding (Hallucination Prevention): To ensure the LLM does not fabricate medical advice, the Medical Knowledge Retrieval Agent queries a local FAISS vector database containing certified WHO guidelines and PubMed medical literature snippets before generating a response.

3. Multi-Agent Division of Labor: Rather than relying on a single prompt, dedicated agents handle OCR, drug safety rules engines, and risk thresholds independently, ensuring modular debugging and clinical precision.
